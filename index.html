01 / THE BIG PICTURE

Density altitude isn't just a number. It's a performance story.

At high-elevation airports, a warm day can make a familiar airplane perform very differently. KochChart turns that concept into practical, visual comparisons you can explore and learn from.

Originally inspired by the classic Koch Chart nomograph, the software now brings together multiple estimation methods, METAR weather, and clear graphical displays.

iEducational use only. KochChart is not approved for operational flight planning. Use your aircraft's AFM/POH for performance decisions.

06 / WHY KOCHCHART?

Created by a pilot.
For fellow pilots.

KochChart began as a digital interpretation of the traditional Koch Chart and evolved into a broader educational tool to illustrate how atmospheric and runway conditions influence light aircraft performance.

It's designed to support learning and awareness—not to replace approved aircraft performance data or required preflight planning.

Plus registration information →

Educational use & licensing

Not for flight planning or operational decisions. KochChart does not satisfy the requirements of FAR 91.103 or other regulatory requirements. Always consult the approved Airplane Flight Manual (AFM) or Pilot's Operating Handbook (POH) for your aircraft's performance calculations. KochChart is intended for light, single-engine, normally aspirated piston aircraft and does not apply to all aircraft types.
